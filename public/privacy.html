Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Website: https://music.khai99.id.vn · Tiếng Việt bên dưới

Chillwith3A is a small, free, non-commercial website that lets a private group of friends queue YouTube songs for a shared room and chat with each other. This policy explains what information the website handles and how.

YouTube API Services

Chillwith3A uses YouTube API Services to search for videos and to look up video details (title, channel name, thumbnail, duration). Videos are played only through the official YouTube embedded player. By using Chillwith3A you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information Google collects through YouTube is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we collect and store

  • Display name you type when joining, shown next to the songs you add and your chat messages.
  • Chat messages and reactions you send, visible to everyone in the room.
  • Likes / dislikes (👍/👎) on the song that is playing, and a running score per display name of the person who chose each song, shown as a small leaderboard and name effects. The room admin can reset it at any time.
  • Songs you add to the queue: the YouTube video ID, title, channel name, thumbnail URL and duration returned by the YouTube API, together with your display name.
  • A random device identifier generated in your browser so the website can recognise your own songs and messages. The server only stores a one-way hash of it.
  • Search terms you enter are sent to the YouTube API to get results. Results are cached in server memory for at most 6 hours so repeated searches do not use extra quota; search terms are not linked to your name and are not stored permanently.

We do not ask for your email, phone number or any account login, we do not use Google/YouTube sign-in (OAuth), and we do not access any YouTube account data.

How long we keep it

  • Chat messages (the most recent 100) and the list of recently played songs (the most recent 20) are kept so people who join later can catch up, and are automatically deleted after 30 days at the latest.
  • Songs waiting in the queue are kept until they are played or removed.
  • The room admin can delete the queue or the whole chat at any time.

Browser storage and cookies

Chillwith3A does not set advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only to remember your display name, room code, device identifier and volume preference on your own device; you can clear it at any time in your browser settings. Video thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's image servers, and the host's page uses the YouTube embedded player, which may set cookies according to Google's own policies.

How information is shared

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Your display name, chat messages and the songs you add are visible to other people using the website. Data is processed by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, which may keep short-lived technical request logs (such as IP address and time) for security and reliability. Your IP address is also used briefly in memory to limit abuse (for example, too many searches per minute) and is not stored.

Your choices

You can remove songs you added from the queue at any time. To ask for your chat messages or other information to be deleted, contact us at the email below and we will remove it.
